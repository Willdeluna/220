Core Concept

ATA 23 is a digital communication network→Voice, data, tuning, and audio are managed through central digital systems.
RIU = main communication hub→If comms are weird, start by thinking RIU and routing.big idea
Radios are endpoints, not the whole system→Many “radio” problems are actually selection, audio, or routing problems.
🔀

System Architecture

Main flow: radios → RIU → AIS/ACP → crew→The RIU routes audio and tuning commands before the crew hears anything.
DMC path supports displays, FMS, and data→Communication data can feed more than just headsets.
Two RIUs provide redundancy→The system steps down instead of completely failing.
🎛️

Radio Management System

RMS controls COM and NAV radios→Radio tuning is centralized instead of being one simple box.
CTP is the primary tuning control→Control Tuning Panel is usually the first place to verify selection.
RTSA and FMS auto-tuning add backup paths→Multiple tuning methods are redundancy, not preference.MX clue
🔌

Radio Tuning Ports

Each radio has three tuning ports→Port B is primary, Port A is secondary, and Port C is tertiary backup.
Failure logic steps down B → A → C→Loss of one path does not mean loss of the radio.important
Both CTPs OFF can force 121.5 MHz→Emergency reversion protects basic communication capability.
📻

VHF System

Three VHF radios are installed→VHF 1 and 2 support voice; VHF 3 often supports data link and ACARS.
PTT times out after long transmission→This prevents a stuck mic from blocking ATC frequency.
VHF faults can be protective shutdowns→Overheat, VSWR, antenna/cable, memory, and test faults can trigger protection.
🎧

AIS / ACP Audio

AIS mixes and distributes all audio→Audio issues do not automatically mean radio failure.
ACP is the crew interface→Select radio, adjust volume, and choose the transmit source.
Only one transmit source at a time→Wrong ACP selection can look exactly like “no comm.”common write-up
🎙️

PTT & Interphone

PTT inputs come from several places→Sidestick, CCP, hand mic, and ACP paths can all matter.
No transmit? Check PTT path first→The radio may be fine while the keying path is bad.line MX
Flight and service interphone support crew and MX→Service interphone is important during ground ops; MECH CALL alerts the nose gear area.
📲

SELCAL / ACARS / CPDLC

SELCAL alerts the aircraft without constant listening→RIU decodes tones and triggers light, tone, or CAS message.
ACARS uses VHF 3 or SATCOM→Weather, dispatch messages, and clearances can move without voice.
CPDLC is text-based ATC communication→Problems are often network/routing issues, not the radio itself.
🎚️

CVR & ELT

CVR records cockpit audio and data link messages→Operation depends on power and trigger logic.
RIPS provides backup power after power loss→Backup recording can continue briefly after aircraft power is lost.
ELT is self-contained emergency equipment→G-switch impact activation, 121.5/406 MHz broadcast, and battery condition matter.
🔧

Troubleshooting Flow

1. Check selection→ACP selected to the correct radio? Volume up? Correct transmit source?
2. Check tuning path→CTP/RTSA/FMS tuning valid? Is the system on Port B, A, or C?
3. Check PTT and RIU logic→If transmit or receive is wrong, follow the path before replacing the radio.use this order
