Core Concept — ATA 28

A220 fuel = computer-controlled fuel management→You are troubleshooting logic, not just pumps and valves.
FQC is the system brain→It controls distribution, balancing, transfer, refuel/defuel, and protection.start here
Sensors drive system behavior→Wrong data can create wrong pump, valve, or quantity behavior.
🛢️

Fuel Storage

Three main tanks→Left wing, right wing, and center tank.
Collector tanks feed engines→The collector tank is where engine feed actually comes from.
Dry bays and baffles matter→They help control leak risk and fuel movement during maneuvers.
🌬️

Vent System

Maintains tank pressure near atmospheric→Prevents tank structural damage from overpressure or vacuum.
Surge tanks, vent tubes, float valves→These parts let tanks breathe safely.
Flame arrestors are safety protection→The vent system is a safety system, not just airflow.safety system
⚙️

Engine Feed System

Each engine has independent feed→Left and right feed paths provide redundancy.
Ejector pump = primary source→Uses motive flow and has no moving parts.normal feed
Boost pump = support and backup→Runs for low pressure, APU use, engine start, and imbalance logic.
🔁

Feed Redundancy

System keeps trying to feed engines→Either tank or pump logic can support continued feed in degraded conditions.
Check valves prevent backflow→They protect pressure and stop fuel moving the wrong way.
Suction feed is limited backup→Available only in degraded conditions and altitude-limited.
⛽

Auto Fuel Transfer

Center tank empties first→Fuel transfer happens automatically with no pilot input.
Transfer starts around wing tanks at 83%→Stops around 86% to maintain balance and CG logic.auto logic
Transfer ejectors and float valves do the work→The FQC commands logic; the plumbing moves the fuel.
⚖️

Imbalance Correction

Imbalance correction is automatic→At roughly 400 lb imbalance, the heavier side pump is commanded on.
Both engines feed from heavier side→Stops when balance gets close again, around 100 lb.
Around 800 lb triggers caution→The system gives the crew a message when imbalance gets too high.
🎛️

Boost Pump Logic

AUTO / ON / OFF modes→AUTO lets the FQC command the pump when conditions require it.
Pump running does not always mean failure→It may be responding to low pressure, start, APU, or imbalance logic.don’t chase ghosts
Manual selection can override logic→Always check switch position and current system mode.
🔥

Engine / APU Protection

Engine feed shutoff valves close during fire→Fuel flow stops as part of ATA 26 fire isolation logic.
APU feeds from left engine feed line→APU fuel SOV closes for APU off, fire, or shutdown logic.
Fuel shutoff is primary protection→Stopping fuel flow is one of the fastest ways to control fire risk.
🔄

Manual Transfer / Crossfeed

Manual transfer through FUEL panel→Allows tank-to-tank, tank-to-center, or gravity transfer.
Gravity transfer equalizes tanks→Uses valve logic instead of pump-driven transfer.
Manual transfer overrides automatic logic→Know the commanded mode before calling a component bad.
🔍

Fuel Leak Logic

No direct leak sensor→The system detects leaks indirectly through calculated logic.
Compares fuel flow and expected use→Also compares left/right consumption patterns.
FUEL LEAK SUSPECT = logic clue→Follow the data before assuming a physical leak location.
🛠️

Refuel / Defuel

Single-point pressure refueling→Controlled by RDCP panel and FQC logic.
Refuel valves open and close automatically→Auto mode stops at preselect quantity.
High-level probes prevent overfill→Protection activates near the upper tank limit.overfill protection
🔋

Refuel Power / Defuel Logic

Opening refuel panel door powers emergency DC logic→Refuel capability remains available without full aircraft power.
Pressure defuel uses boost pumps→Suction defuel uses the truck.
Center tank transfers into wings first→Center tank is not normally suctioned directly.
📊

Fuel Quantity Indication

Capacitance probes measure fuel quantity→Fuel is measured electrically, not visually.
RDCs send data to FQC→FQC calculates quantity, temperature, and density compensation.
Redundant channels and probe lanes→The system usually degrades instead of fully failing.
⚠️

Warnings & Sensors

Low fuel warning around 30 minutes remaining→Use warnings with fuel synoptic and quantity logic.
Fuel temperature displayed on synoptic→Important for fuel freezing and operating limits.
Imbalance warnings around 800 lb→High imbalance means automatic correction was not enough or needs attention.
🔗

System Interaction

ATA 28 crosses multiple systems→Fuel faults may interact with APU, fire protection, electrical, and engine systems.
ATA 26 controls fire shutoff→Fire isolation can create fuel system effects.
ATA 24 powers the logic→Electrical faults can look like fuel faults.cross ATA
🔧

Maintenance Reality / Troubleshooting Flow

Most issues are sensors, pressure switches, valves, or logic→Complete pump failure is not always the first suspect.
Start with FUEL synoptic and CAS messages→Then check auto transfer, pump logic, valve state, and sensor data.
Follow: Data → Logic → Flow → Component→Do not replace parts until you understand what the FQC is commanding.line MX flow
